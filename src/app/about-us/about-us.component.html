We have 25+ Years of Experience.

Sa Shien Shito Ryu Karate Do India is a proud associate member of the Karate India Organisation (KIO), the only body in India affiliated with the World Karate Federation (WKF), Asian Karate Federation (AKF), Commonwealth Karate Federation (CKF), and South Asian Karate Do Federation (SAKF). Recognized by the International Olympic Committee (IOC), our organisation upholds the highest global standards of karate training and practice.

Headed by Mr. M. K. Sasikumar, we are dedicated to promoting the art and discipline of Shito Ryu Karate across India. Our mission is to instill not only self-defense skills but also values of respect, discipline, perseverance, and leadership among our students.

With recognition and membership valid through 2025, Sa Shien Shito Ryu Karate Do India stands as a trusted and authentic platform for karatekas to train, compete, and grow—whether at the grassroots or international level.

We believe in empowering individuals, especially the youth, to achieve excellence in both martial arts and life. Through structured programs, workshops, and competitions, we aim to develop strong, confident, and disciplined individuals who can proudly represent India in the global karate arena.
