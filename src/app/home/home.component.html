Our Services

Training Programs

Karate
Karate is a traditional Japanese martial art focusing on striking techniques like punches, kicks, and blocks. It enhances discipline, strength, and self-defense skills.

Silambam
Silambam is an ancient Indian martial art using bamboo sticks and swift footwork. It builds agility, coordination, and traditional weapon-based defense skills.

Tai chi
Tai Chi is a Chinese martial art known for its slow, flowing movements and focus on breath control. It promotes balance, flexibility, and inner peace for your mind and body.

Wushu
Wushu is a modern exhibition and full-contact sport derived from traditional Chinese martial arts. It combines fluid movements, acrobatics, and combat techniques.

Kobudo
Kobudo is an Okinawan martial art specializing in traditional weapons like the bo, sai, and nunchaku. It emphasizes precision, discipline, and heritage-based training.

Self Defence
Self-defense training equips individuals with techniques to protect themselves in threatening situations. It boosts awareness, confidence, and physical readiness.
